# Lighthouse Guard privacy policy

_Effective date: 27 September 2026_

Lighthouse Guard is an app published by Damien Dubernet that controls SteamVR Base Station 2.0 units over Bluetooth. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Lighthouse Guard does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server: the app never sends anything over the internet.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find the base stations near you and to send them commands (on, standby, sleep, identify) or read their details (channel, firmware, serial number). This communication happens directly between your device and your base stations. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

- On iOS, the app asks for Bluetooth access the first time you search for stations.
- On Android, the app asks for the Nearby devices permission. It does not use your location.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things on your device only:

- the names you give your base stations and the groups you create;
- your settings (theme, accent colour, app icon, language, display and scan options).

This information never leaves your device unless you use Settings → Data → Export settings yourself. The backup file is shared only where you choose to send it.

You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Data → Reset everything, or by uninstalling the app.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under their own privacy policies. The developer does not receive any information that identifies you.

## Children

The app does not collect any data, including from children.
